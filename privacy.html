Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The cliperrino upload tool is used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes solely to upload videos and confirm the channel.

The tool does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other users. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
